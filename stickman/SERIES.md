# StickWise — Series plan

Upload alternating A / B, one Short per day. After 4–6 episodes compare **Viewed vs Swiped Away** in YouTube Studio
and double down on the stronger series. Each series = one YouTube playlist.

## Series format (all episodes)
- `seriesBadge(series, ep, t)` top-left for the whole video.
- 30–40 s. Hook in the first 2 s, frame 0 = full hook layout.
- Structure: hook → relatable situation → why your brain/history did it → name of the effect/lesson → one fix/twist → last line loops back to the hook.
- No long outro: "EP N+1 TOMORROW →" text over the last scene, then dissolve into the frame-0 layout (seamless loop).
- YouTube title pattern: `<Title> <emoji> #<SeriesTag> #shorts`.
