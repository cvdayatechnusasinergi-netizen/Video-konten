This guy paid over a billion dollars… for two pizzas.
May 2010. Bitcoin is brand new, and basically worth nothing. Like, internet Monopoly money.
A programmer named Laszlo posts on a forum: "I'll pay ten thousand Bitcoin for two large pizzas."
A few days later, someone takes the deal. Two pizzas show up at his door. Total value? About forty-one bucks.
Then Bitcoin went… a little crazy.
At its peak, those ten thousand Bitcoin were worth over one point two billion dollars.
That's not dinner. That's a fleet of private jets.
But here's the twist. Laszlo says he doesn't regret it. Someone had to prove Bitcoin could buy real stuff. Today, May twenty-second is literally called Bitcoin Pizza Day.
The real lesson? Every dollar you spend has a hidden price tag: what it could have become. That's called opportunity cost.
