Kodak invented the digital camera. And didn't want it.
1975. A Kodak engineer named Steve Sasson builds the first one. Eight pounds. Twenty-three seconds per photo.
His bosses' reaction, according to Sasson? That's cute. But don't tell anyone.
Because Kodak made a fortune selling film. And digital cameras don't need film.
Kodak did go digital… eventually. But everyone else moved faster.
In 2012, Kodak filed for bankruptcy.
