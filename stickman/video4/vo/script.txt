Ever notice the large popcorn is always just fifty cents more?
Small, three bucks. Medium, six fifty. Large, seven. Easy choice, right?
Here's the thing: the medium isn't there to be bought.
It's a decoy. Its only job is to make the large look like a steal.
One experiment offered students three options. Eighty-four percent took the combo.
Remove the option nobody wanted… and it dropped to thirty-two.
Coffee sizes, streaming plans, phone plans. Once you see it, you can't unsee it.
The fix? Cover the middle option. Then ask: do I actually want more?
So… still going large?
