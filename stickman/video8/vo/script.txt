You got a raise. So why are you still broke?
Even one in three people making over five hundred grand a year say they live paycheck to paycheck.
Here's where the raise goes. Fancier coffee. One more subscription. Delivery instead of cooking. A new phone.
Add it up… and the raise is gone.
It's called lifestyle creep.
Your brain gets used to nice things fast. Yesterday's luxury becomes today's normal.
The fix? Before you upgrade anything, save half the raise.
