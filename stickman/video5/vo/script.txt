In 2000, Blockbuster could have bought Netflix… for fifty million dollars.
Back then, Blockbuster had nine thousand stores. Netflix? A tiny startup mailing DVDs, and losing money.
So the founders flew to Dallas and offered to sell. Fifty million.
The CEO was trying not to laugh. Too niche, he said.
Ten years later, Blockbuster went bankrupt.
And Netflix? Worth around two hundred sixty-five billion dollars.
Today, there's exactly one Blockbuster left. One.
The thing you laugh at today… might replace you tomorrow. So, what are you laughing at right now?
