Same five dollars. But your brain treats them totally different.
Coffee? Instant yes. Every single morning.
But a five dollar app? Suddenly you're reading reviews for ten minutes.
That's because your brain has invisible wallets. One for treats, one for bills, and one for "silly stuff."
Psychologists call it mental accounting.
And here's the scary part. Five bucks a day is over eighteen hundred dollars a year.
That's three hundred sixty-five apps.
The fix? Pretend every dollar comes out of the same wallet.
So… still think that coffee is cheap?
