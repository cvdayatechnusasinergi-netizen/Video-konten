This man gave up ten percent of Apple… for eight hundred dollars.
1976. Apple has three founders. You know two of them.
Ron Wayne wrote the contract, drew the first logo, and got ten percent.
Twelve days later… he panics.
The Steves had nothing to lose. Ron had a house, a car, savings. If Apple failed, creditors would come after him.
So he walked away. Eight hundred bucks. Later, fifteen hundred more.
Today, that ten percent would be worth around four hundred billion dollars.
His take at ninety-one? No regrets.
Sometimes the safest move is the most expensive one. Would you have taken the eight hundred?
